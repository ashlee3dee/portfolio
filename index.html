Hi, I’m Ashlee. I turn sci-fi ideas into 3D worlds, and technical challenges into tools for artists.

I'm a self-taught technical artist and 3D generalist with a background in additive manufacturing and software engineering. My work brings together procedural thinking, visual experimentation, and a love for environments that feel like somewhere else.

I’ve worked on projects for Warner Brothers/DC, Honkai: Star Rail, and Nike. From lighting and shaders to custom tools, I like making the complicated feel possible.

Primary toolkit

Blender / Geometry Nodes / Substance Designer / Unity Shader Graph & VFX Graph / Unreal Blueprints
